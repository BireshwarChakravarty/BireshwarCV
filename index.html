Profile

Sixteen years.
Built to lead at scale.

Turning policy into stories, mandates into movements, and national ambitions into 10.8 billion moments of attention a year, with a filmmaker's eye for what makes people stop, look, and care.

Digital and integrated communications leader driving large-scale government and enterprise mandates across the public sector, B2B, and consumer brands. At Avian We., steering digital strategy for national ministries: a monthly mandate of ~INR 1.16 Crore, an aggregate annual portfolio of ~INR 13.9 Crores, and an 80-member cross-functional team delivering a national ecosystem of 10.8 billion annual impressions across MIB, MEA, MoPSW, NHAI and RITES.

Former P&L owner of an agency vertical at popkorn generating INR 95 Lakhs/month, delivering 19% revenue growth and launching a dedicated AI Marketing practice that improved productivity by up to 28%. Founded MRKTNG Labs India from the ground up. Fluent in campaign conceptualisation, production, and client servicing, with a rare foundation in film, photography, and visual storytelling.

Award-winning filmmaker: Best Short Film, International Short Film Festival, Phoenix AZ, and an Official Selection at Goa Film Festival 2015. Commissioned photographer for Bharat Dalmia Group, featured by the National Museum. Collaborated with Harley-Davidson, Ferrari, and Lamborghini on branded content.
